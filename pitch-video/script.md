# Beyond: 3-minute pitch video script

The video `beyond-pitch-3min.mp4` is exactly 3:00, 1920×1080, with a silent audio track to record over.

- **0:00–1:00:** the animated intro, with the core message up front.
- **1:00–2:54:** a live demo of the Beyond prototype, with a caption bar naming each step.
- **2:54–3:00:** the closing slide.

The lines below are timed to the picture, about 150 words a minute. Things appear on screen as you say them.

---

## Part A · Intro (0:00–1:00)

**0:00–0:11 · Hook**
What if the best person for the job has never held the title?
Meet Kimi. He taught himself customer success, but without three years in SaaS, a CV filter rejects him.
*Your past shouldn't define your future.*

**0:11–0:22 · Problem**
Companies describe past profiles. CV filters reject career changers. Potential gets missed.
And with AI, every application looks polished, so a tailored letter now predicts a callback 52 percent less.

**0:22–0:30 · Research**
Changing the job ad isn't enough. And every extra hoop costs you: 36 percent of candidates quit.
We need to change the interaction.

**0:30–0:41 · Beyond**
That's Beyond. Two parts: a prescreen, then matching.
Refine the job, apply, choose, meet for eight minutes, and continue.

**0:41–0:55 · Prescreen**
Every event sets its bar. Candidates share their evidence, or qualify through a task or a recommendation.
AI summarises it. A person decides, and AI never rejects anyone.

**0:55–1:00 · End card**
AI summarises, people decide. Let me show you.

---

## Part B · Live demo (1:00–2:54)

**1:00–1:22 · Kimi joins an Espoo event** *(caption: Part 1 · Prescreen)*
Here's Kimi joining TalentMatch Espoo. He picks the work he's interested in, and how he's applying: a referral from his former manager, his experience so far, and the event's short video task.
His route counts too, including what he taught himself: a customer success course, data basics and AI tools.

**1:22–1:45 · The Espoo team reviews every application**
Now we're the Espoo team. Kimi's application comes with a background summary: his roles, his self-taught learning, and the evidence behind each path.
The AI only summarises. A person reads it and accepts him into the event.

**1:45–2:02 · Northstar Software refines the job**
Next, the company. Instead of "three years in SaaS", Northstar describes the real work: the impact, the tasks and what can be learned on the job.
The only true requirement left is English for customer conversations.

**2:02–2:23 · Kimi applies**
Kimi applies with no CV screening. He answers one question: why this job, and why him.
Then a ten-minute pre-task: onboarding takes 14 days, how would you start? He'd talk to customers who quit, then fix the cheapest part first.

**2:23–2:33 · The company reads every answer and invites**
The company reads every answer. Applicants are listed newest first and never ranked. They invite Kimi.

**2:33–2:54 · Meet: eight minutes, one real problem**
Then the eight-minute conversation around one real problem. The company rates what they saw, three things on their own scales, never added up, and writes a short note.
A candidate a CV filter would have rejected is now in the process.

---

## Part C · Close (2:54–3:00)

Your past should inform your future, not limit it. Thank you.
