# Beyond: Recruitment for the Future, Not by the Past

AI is changing what people do for work, but recruitment still judges them mostly by what they've done before. Career changers with real potential get filtered out by job titles and years of experience. AI-written applications have also made polished CVs and cover letters an even weaker signal.

Beyond is a matchmaking flow for Espoo recruitment events. It replaces CV screening with real human interaction, in two parts.

## Part 1: Prescreen

Each event sets its own entry level, and there is more than one way to meet it.

1. **Join the event.** Candidates say what work interests them and choose how they're applying:
   - a referral from someone who knows their work
   - their previous experience, including what they've learned on their own
   - a degree in any field
   - the event's short assignment, for example a video

   They share only the links and files they choose, such as LinkedIn, a portfolio or a certificate.
2. **Espoo review.** AI summarises each application against the event's entry level. A person on the Espoo team reads it and decides who joins. AI never rejects anyone. Candidates who don't fit are pointed to other events or Espoo's employment services.

## Part 2: Matching

3. **Refine the job.** The company describes the work that actually needs doing, what can be learned after joining, and only the requirements that are genuinely needed.
4. **Apply.** Instead of sending a CV, candidates answer "Why this opportunity, and why you?" and complete a 10-minute pre-task.
5. **Choose.** The company reads every answer and decides whom to invite. Applicants are listed newest first and never ranked.
6. **Meet.** An 8-minute conversation around one real problem. The company notes three observable behaviours: understanding the problem, explaining their thinking, and adapting to new information. Ratings are never added up or compared.
7. **Continue.** The company continues its own recruitment process with the candidates it selects.

## Principles

- **AI summarises, people decide.** AI helps companies define the job and helps the Espoo team read applications. It never scores, ranks, filters or rejects anyone.
- **Candidates share their own evidence.** Beyond uses only what candidates choose to share, with their consent, rather than searching the web behind their back.
- **Little is asked before the company commits.** Long, hoop-filled processes make candidates drop out. Prescreening happens once per event, not once per job.

## Pilot

We propose piloting Beyond alongside existing Espoo matchmaking events. We'd measure:

- better-defined opportunities
- meaningful conversations
- how many candidates are invited to continue
- whether career changers find relevant connections

*Your past should inform your future, not limit it.*

---

**One-line version:** Beyond is a matchmaking flow for Espoo recruitment events. Each event sets its bar with several ways in, and then a short pre-task and an 8-minute problem-solving conversation replace CV screening, so companies find candidates by their potential rather than their past job titles.
